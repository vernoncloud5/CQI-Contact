About CQI

Built on the cleaning trade. Run like a modern contractor.

Cloud Quantum Innovations grew out of a family that made its living in the cleaning industry. That's not a marketing line — it's where the standards come from. We know what "clean" actually means when an inspector is looking, and we know that in facility services the details are the business.

Today CQI brings that hands-on foundation into federal contracting: an S-Corp small disadvantaged business that primes facility-services contracts and builds the systems, subcontractor relationships, and continuity plans to deliver them dependably at military and federal installations.

The name says the approach — cloud for reach and flexibility, quantum for precision, innovations for the way we solve problems others treat as too complex.
